*Datenschutzerklärung.* This page explains which personal data is processed when you visit this website.

## Who is responsible

Philipp Mundhenk, e-mail: [contact@mundhenk.org](mailto:contact@mundhenk.org)

## What this website does not do

- It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.
- It has no comments, no contact forms and no user accounts.
- All styles, fonts, images and the small search script are served from this website itself; no third-party content is loaded while you read.

## Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.
According to GitHub, "when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes".
I have no access to these logs.
The legal basis is my legitimate interest in delivering this website securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
GitHub may process this data in the USA; details are in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## E-mail

If you write to me, I use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Links to other websites

Links to other sites (for example GitHub, GitHub Sponsors, LinkedIn, the share links below posts, or shops) are plain links.
No data is sent to these sites unless you click a link; after that, their own privacy policies apply.

## Search

The search runs entirely in your browser on an index loaded from this website; search terms are not sent anywhere.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
